My Philosophy

I believe technology should be both innovative and transparent. My philosophy centers on ethical innovation, inclusivity, and building tools that empower rather than replace human intelligence.

The future of AI is collaborative — not competitive. I think of it this way:

The right equation Output = You + Max(AI)
not
The wrong one Output = Max(You, AI)

Technology should amplify human potential, not overshadow it.
